Demo sources & protocols

These research visualizations are not clinical assessments.

Control Cohort comparison

Animation source: Echo4DIR. The original frame-by-frame comparator is relabeled “w/o TC-RSA”; mesh imagery and timing are unchanged. This is not a newly run ablation. The MIA25 baseline is Laumer et al., Medical Image Analysis 101:103434 (2025).

Motion examples and display views

The MI card uses MRI-derived masks with an MI LV prior, not a verified clinical MI echo acquisition. The A2C/A3C/A4C selectors show schematic vertical-axis rotations, not calibrated acquisition planes. Playback is normalized, not clinical heart rate. The epicardial envelope does not independently establish myocardial wall thickness.

Held-out shape adaptation

A2C is excluded from the shape encoder and shape-update loss but used for probe-pose calibration and evaluation. It is therefore held out from shape adaptation, not completely untouched. Backgrounds are matched real ultrasound frames with peripheral scanner annotations removed. Section 04 instead displays observed-view fitting.

Whole-heart display

P1's labeled display copy shifts RA downward by 3% of whole-heart height at every phase. This is an illustrative adjustment, not an evaluated reconstruction. The Original button restores unchanged geometry. P2 is unchanged. Independent closed chamber surfaces do not guarantee valve connectivity.

CT comparison

These static end-diastolic surfaces rotate only for viewing. Global similarity registration adjusts position, orientation and uniform scale, without local deformation. Revised-label examples are complementary comparisons, not additional independent validation or evidence of absolute scale recovery. The blue wireframe is simplified only for display. Mask insets: yellow = echo, pink = reconstruction, cyan = CT.
